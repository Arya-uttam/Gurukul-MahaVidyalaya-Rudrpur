## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the synthesis of ancient Vedic erudition and rigorous contemporary higher education. Designed specifically for higher learning at Gurukul Snatakottar Mahavidyalaya Rudrapur, the aesthetic reconciles three core pillars: spiritual purity (*Sadhana* & *Yagya*), academic mastery (*Vidya*), and martial-physical discipline (*Vyayama* & traditional sports like *Kushti* and *Mallakhambh*).

### Personality & Values
- **Dignified & Venerable:** Anchored in sacred lineage, radiating stability, reverence, and ethical depth without feeling archaic or dusty.
- **Vibrant & Disciplined:** Reflected through warm, living ochres, fiery saffrons, and structured, razor-sharp typographic cadences.
- **Harmonious Synthesis:** Seamlessly bridging Sanskrit and Hindi scripts with English nomenclature for global academic standards.

### Aesthetic Movement
The style is **Warm Editorial Classical** overlaid with **Sacred Structural Geometries**:
- Surfaces emulate aged parchment, sandstone architectural panels, and subtle unlacquered silk textures rather than sterile synthetic plastic.
- Accents deploy metallic Vedic brass and gold foil delineations, grounding modern digital primitives in ceremonial prestige.
- UI elements prioritize breath, balance, and centered classical alignment for formal institutional moments, paired with dense, readable structures for curriculum tables and admissions registers.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from sacred rituals, Ayurvedic botanicals, and traditional sandstone architecture:

- **Sacred Kesari & Agni (Primary - `#E65100` / `#F57C00`):** Symbolizes the living sacrificial flame (*Yagya*), truth, and intellectual awakening. Applied to primary calls to action, active navigation tabs, and focal badges.
- **Deep Kumkum & Terracotta (Secondary - `#B71C1C`):** Resonant with solemnity, strength, auspiciousness, and athletic valor. Used for important institutional announcements, critical headers, and formal seals.
- **Vedic Gold & Patina Bronze (Tertiary - `#D4AF37` / `#9A7B2C`):** Denotes purity, scholarship, and classical honors. Utilized for ceremonial borders, divider insignias, academic medals, and high-tier credential cards.
- **Tulsi & Forest Herbal Green (Accent - `#2E7D32`):** Reflects organic life, campus forestry, holistic wellness, and calm rejuvenation. Reserved for status badges (such as verified admissions or athletic records), success states, and natural sanctuary features.
- **Sandstone & Ivory Vellum (Base Neutral Scales):** 
  - Canvas Surface: `#FFFDF9`
  - Container / Layered Card Surface: `#FBF7EE`
  - Inset / Borders / Shading Surface: `#F5EBE1`
- **Charcoal Bhashma & Roasted Umber (Typography Neutrals):**
  - High-Contrast Text: `#2C221E` (Warm charcoal-brown rather than stark artificial black)
  - Muted Inscription Text: `#64554E`

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances the gravitas of ceremonial script with utilitarian legibility for dual-language (Hindi and English) environments:

- **Primary Display & Headings (`Noto Serif`):** Chosen for its exquisite glyph support in both Devanagari and Latin scripts. Its serifs possess calligraphic chiseled terminals that honor stone inscriptions, Sanskrit manuscripts, and scholarly charters.
- **Interface & Body Engine (`Inter`):** Delivers uncompromising clarity across small displays, dense grade sheets, application forms, and administrative logs.
- **Sloka & Chanting Treatment:** When rendering Devanagari slokas, mantras, or institutional aphorisms, apply the `sloka-quote` style with subtle centered alignment, warm terracotta tinting (`#B71C1C`), and a pair of traditional Indian punctuation double-dandas (*॥*).
- **Label Capitalization:** English labels and metadata badges use tracked uppercase (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`), evoking carved brass plaques.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the harmonic symmetry and sacred geometry of Vedic Mandir architecture: deliberate central axes, measured rhythms, and disciplined grid alignments.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers are capped at an optimal reading width of `1280px`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Complex data (such as hostel routines or athletic regimens) reflows into single stacked vertical streams.

### Spacing Rhythm
- Sectional spacing follows spacious multi-step multiples (`space-xl` to `space-xl * 2`) to instill a tranquil, contemplative reading pace.
- Micro-interactions (form groups, label pairings) are locked tightly with `space-xs` and `space-sm` for immediate visual association.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on synthetic, floaty shadows. Instead, it employs **Tonal Layering**, **Warm Sandstone Ambient Glows**, and **Gold Filigree Tracing**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** Pure vellum tint (`#FFFDF9`) forming the peaceful base plate.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card & Module Layer):** `#FBF7EE` framed with a subtle 1px border of `#F5EBE1`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Floating Modals & Interactive Menus):** Pure white background with an ambient shadow tinted by burnt sienna: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(44, 34, 30, 0.08), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(44, 34, 30, 0.04)`.
- **Prestige Embellishment (Ceremonial Depth):** Featured accolade cards and degree program headers employ a dual-border treatment: an outer boundary in `#D4AF37` at 25% opacity, combined with a 1px solid inset border in `#D4AF37`, creating the visual presence of framed diplomas or brass plaques.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **soft, architectural geometry** (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Standard components (buttons, text inputs, academic cards) feature a conservative `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, recalling precisely dressed sandstone blocks.
- Medium containers and institutional badges use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Pill shapes are strictly forbidden for structural buttons to maintain solemn institutional authority; pills are reserved solely for live status markers (such as active meditation session indicators or hostel admission state tags).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Agni Action):** Deep Kesari saffron background (`#E65100`), white text, crisp 4px corners, and an inset subtle border (`1px solid #C44300`). Hover elevates warmth to `#F57C00` accompanied by an imperceptible ambient saffron glow.
- **Secondary (Mahavidyalaya Regalia):** Deep Terracotta/Kumkum red (`#B71C1C`), white text, reserved for solemn applications like "Download Prospectus" or "Vedic Convocation."
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Sandstone base with a 1px border in Vedic Bronze (`#9A7B2C`), text in `#2C221E`.

### Cards & Parchment Panels
- Academic and residential cards use a `#FBF7EE` foundation with a 1px `#F5EBE1` border.
- **Honorific Cards (Yoga Acharyas, Wrestling Champions):** Incorporate a subtle top accent bar in Gold Foil (`#D4AF37`, 3px height) or a delicate top-centered *Om* / lotus filigree divider.

### Badges & Chips
- Compact, squared-off forms with uppercase typography (`label-sm`).
- **Athletic Excellence (Vyayama):** Deep saffron background with golden text for achievements in Kushti, Gymnastics, and Mallakhambh.
- **Vedic Scholarship:** Sandstone background with Terracotta red border and text for Sanskrit Sahitya and Darshana credentials.
- **Holistic Living:** Herbal Green (`#2E7D32`) background with pale cream text for Ashram guidelines and organic dietary notices.

### Input Fields
- Structured around ivory backgrounds (`#FFFDF9`) with crisp 1px borders in `#E3D5C8`. Focus states replace standard browser rings with a warm saffron outline (`#E65100`) accompanied by a 2px sandstone offset ring.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Sharp, dignified inputs rendered in terracotta `#B71C1C` with ivory checks, avoiding playful rounded toggles in favor of academic precision.

### Institutional Sloka Callout
- Full-width or boxed panel with a left border (4px solid `#D4AF37`), subtle warm sandstone wash (`#FBF7EE`), featuring Sanskrit text in `sloka-quote` typography with Hindi and English contextual translations immediately underneath in muted tones.
